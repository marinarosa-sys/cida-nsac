Suggestions (TOPSIS Fuzzy)

What is TOPSIS Fuzzy? It is a multi-criteria decision-making method that combines Fuzzy Logic (translating linguistic priorities like “High” or “Medium” into Triangular Fuzzy Numbers) with TOPSIS (ranking alternatives by their geometric distance to the ideal agronomic scenario and distance away from the worst-case scenario).

Why use it for crop ranking? Orbital telemetry (rainfall, soil moisture, temperature, slope) involves natural climate uncertainty and competing trade-offs. Fuzzy TOPSIS prevents a single extreme variable from distorting the decision and yields a transparent suitability score (0–100%) aligned with your priorities.
