The model behind the app

Built on Recognition-Primed Decision making

Gary Klein studied how experts decide under pressure. They rarely compare every option. They recognize the situation, based on experience, and act on what worked before. CIDA gives farmers that recognition step, backed by NASA data and decades of local history.

Choosing and carrying out the plan stays with the farmer and the local agronomist. CIDA frames and ranks the options, on purpose, and never decides for you.

Choosing the crop

Best option for your priorities, ranked with Fuzzy TOPSIS

You rarely want just one thing. CIDA ranks crop and rotation options against all your priorities at once, using Fuzzy TOPSIS, a multi-criteria method that handles vague judgments like “quite important” and uncertain data without false precision.

1 · PrioritiesYou set the weightsRate what matters, such as water saving, income, soil structure and risk, as words: low, medium, high. Each word becomes a fuzzy number.
2 · ScoresOptions are ratedEach crop option is scored on every criterion from NASA cues, crop traits and your constraints, with a range for uncertainty.
3 · DistanceIdeal vs. worstWe measure how close each option is to the ideal solution and how far it is from the worst one.
4 · RankingA clear orderA closeness score from 0 to 1 ranks the options and shows why. You review the top choices.

Projection and simulation

See the outcome before you plant.

A projection assistant lets you combine crops in a rotation and simulate what could happen, under different rain and temperature scenarios. Results come as ranges with their uncertainty, never as single promises.

  • Combine crops. Build a sequence or an intercrop with two or three crops.
  • Change the scenario. Shift rain and temperature, from a drier year to a hotter one.
  • Compare results. Water use, soil cover, pest-cycle risk and income range, side by side.
  • See the limits. Information gaps and risks are flagged for local review.
